A GET, because it asks for a file and changes nothing. {% comment %}Why the PDF is one person at a time, said where the choice is made rather than in a help panel nobody opens. A PDF of eleven people is eleven documents, and stapling them together makes a thing that cannot be handed to any one of them. Asking for one anyway is answered by the view with a message rather than by a disabled button — a control that refuses without saying why is worse than one that explains.{% endcomment %}
