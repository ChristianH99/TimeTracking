{% comment %}**The people filter.** A tick box each, above the grid rather than in the rows — a control that hides the row it lives in is a control nobody can find again to switch back on. Nothing here is saved and nobody is removed from anything: the page always arrives with everybody showing, and the ticks are how somebody says which two people they are looking at for the next thirty seconds. That is why it is not a form and posts nothing — it is a way of reading the page, not a setting about it. With no script the boxes do nothing and the whole grid is on show, which is the right failure: the page's job is to show who is off, and a filter is an aid to that rather than a part of it.{% endcomment %}
